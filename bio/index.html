London Oliver

London Oliver is a bright rising talent in the film industry. His 2025 improv dating video went viral on Instagram with 2.8 million views. The video was liked by some of the biggest names in film and television, including Zoe Saldana, Olivia Wilde, and Adam Devine. London has starred in short films and commercial work. He resides in Los Angeles, California, and is represented by the Michael Zanuck Agency.
